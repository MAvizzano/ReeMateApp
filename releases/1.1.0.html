Version 1.1

Update ReeMate on your iPhone too. Both apps need version 1.1 to connect. After updating, pair your iPhone again with the QR code or the 6-digit code.

What's New

  • All-new connection: faster, more reliable and encrypted, with a key of its own for every iPhone–Mac pair.
  • Quick pairing: scan the QR code shown on your Mac, or approve a 6-digit code.
  • Works on managed work Macs that can't announce themselves on the network.
  • Stays connected across sleep, wake and Wi-Fi changes.
  • Presentation controls: next, previous and black screen for Keynote and PowerPoint.
  • Automatic updates: ReeMate Server now keeps itself up to date.
  • Simpler menu bar window, with pairing requests shown right there.
  • Bluetooth is no longer needed.

Fixes

  • The cursor no longer drifts past the edge of the screen.
  • "Launch at login" now reports an error when macOS refuses it.
  • Smoother pointer and scrolling, plus stability improvements.
